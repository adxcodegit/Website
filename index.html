Profile

Analyst description

I spent two and a half years at Lifelong Wealth in Mumbai, moving from analyst to senior analyst on a ₹35 Cr discretionary HNI book — asset allocation, AIF and PMS manager due diligence, and the bottom-up equity work underneath it. Roughly 190 bps of annualised outperformance against a blended Nifty 50 benchmark.

Since then, an MSc in Finance at Simon Business School and a remote research seat covering US defence, industrials and aerospace. What I publish here is the sell-side format applied to Indian names: three-statement models, DCF and SOTP, reverse-DCF cross-checks, and a written case that states its own failure points.
